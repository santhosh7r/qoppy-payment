// pipeline

From idea to production in four steps.

Design in the visual builder, deploy to your environment, connect to your stack, ship. No long procurement cycle. No multi-quarter rollout.

step.01

Design

Build workflows visually. Triggers, branches, AI steps, integrations — drag, drop, configure.

step.02

Deploy

One-command install of the Qoppy runtime on your VPS, Docker, or Kubernetes cluster.

step.03

Connect

Authenticate to Microsoft 365, Salesforce, SAP, or any custom API. Tokens stay in your runtime.

step.04

Run

Workflows execute inside your environment. Monitor, iterate, scale — all from the same dashboard.

// built for enterprise

Production-grade from day one.

Speed without compromise. Qoppy was designed for the controls, integrations, and reliability that enterprise IT actually requires.

01 / scale

Built for volume

Run thousands of concurrent workflows on your own infrastructure. Horizontal scaling, queue-based execution, no shared-tenant limits.

02 / integrations

200+ connectors

Native support for Microsoft 365, Graph API, Salesforce, SAP, Oracle, ServiceNow, and a generic REST/SOAP/database connector for everything else.

03 / governance

SSO, RBAC, audit logs

Azure AD / Entra integration, role-based access, complete audit trail of every workflow run, version control, and approval gates.

04 / deployment

Your infrastructure

Cloud, on-prem, or air-gapped. The runtime ships as a container — deploy where your security, latency, and compliance needs require.

05 / data control

Data stays with you

Workflows execute inside your perimeter. Qoppy provides the platform and the AI endpoint — payloads never persist on our infrastructure.

06 / reliability

99.99% uptime SLA

Multi-region failover for the control plane. Your runtime is yours, so its availability is yours to architect — and we help you design it.
